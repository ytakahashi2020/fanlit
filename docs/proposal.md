# Fanlit

_Compressed-NFT ticketing with built-in anti-scalping resale and fan rewards_

## Summary

Fanlit is an event ticketing platform using Solana compressed NFTs so organizers can issue cheap, verifiable tickets, while a built-in resale market enforces price caps and splits royalties with artists. Fans earn loyalty points as NFTs for attending shows, unlocking perks at future events.

## Target users

Independent event organizers, musicians, and their fans who attend live shows

## Problem

Fans face scalped, overpriced tickets and fake duplicates, while organizers and artists get no share of resale value.

## Solution

Compressed NFT tickets enable cheap issuance and transparent resale with enforced price caps and automatic royalty splits to organizers/artists.

## MVP features

- Compressed NFT minting for low-cost ticket issuance at scale
- Built-in resale marketplace with enforced max price and royalty split
- QR-based check-in app verifying NFT ticket ownership on-chain
- Loyalty NFT badges minted to attendees after event check-in
- Organizer dashboard for ticket sales and royalty analytics

## Chains

Solana

## Tech

Metaplex Bubblegum (cNFTs), Anchor, React, Phantom Wallet Adapter, QR scanning library, Helius RPC

## Category

Consumer

## Why now

Compressed NFTs make mass ticket issuance cost-effective for the first time, and anti-scalping demand is high as live event prices surge globally.

## Roadmap

- Pilot with a local indie music venue or event series
- Add secondary market integrations and mobile wallet UX polish
- Expand loyalty program into cross-event fan rewards network
